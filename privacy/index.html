Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Short version: Tubwise collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Tubwise has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your phone

Your tubs, test results, doses, soaks, fill dates, reminders and settings are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. We can’t see them and we have no copy.

The home-screen widget shows your latest test and the days to your next drain. It reads the same data on your phone.

Backups and exports

A backup is one file (JSON). A CSV export is a spreadsheet file. Your phone’s share sheet or file picker sends them where you choose: Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a computer, an email to yourself. Those services handle the file under their own privacy policies. We never receive it.

Phone backups

On Android, if Google device backup is turned on, Android may include Tubwise’s data file in that backup and bring it back on a new phone. Only the data file is included. The record of your Pro purchase stays on the device, so Pro comes back through Restore purchases. Google handles this backup under its own privacy policy.

On iPhone, the app’s data is part of your iCloud or computer backup if you use one, as with most apps. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Network

Tubwise makes no network requests of its own. The only exception is purchases: buying or restoring Pro is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their privacy policies. We don’t receive your payment details. The app only gets the purchase status from the store and saves it on your phone.

Permissions

The only permission Tubwise asks for is notifications, for the reminders you set. They are scheduled on your phone; no push service is involved. No camera (you type strip readings), no location, contacts, microphone or photo access.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you. We don’t share them or add you to a mailing list. Ask at any time and we delete them.

This website

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. The calculators run in your browser; what you type is not sent or stored, and it is gone when you close the page. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Tubwise is made for adults who look after a hot tub. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no app data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To delete everything in the app, uninstall it: the data exists only on your phone and in backup files you saved. For emails you sent us, contact us and we delete them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Contact: hello@mufatech.com
