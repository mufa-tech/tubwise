Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. Anything else: write to us, a person answers.

How do I back up my tests?

In the app, open More → Your data → Back up. Tubwise saves everything (tubs, tests, doses, soaks, reminders and settings) into one file and opens your phone’s share sheet. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a computer or an email to yourself. We never see the file.

Make a fresh backup before you change phones or reset one.

How do I restore a backup, or move to a new phone?

Install Tubwise on the new phone, open More → Your data → Restore from a backup and pick the file. It replaces what is on that phone; you can undo right after. Then restore your purchase (next question) to get Pro back.

How do I restore Pro?

Open More → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. A purchase on the App Store does not carry over to Google Play, or the other way round; the two stores keep separate records.

How does the free trial work?

You can try Pro free for 7 days, once. On iPhone the trial is a free item in the App Store; on Android it starts inside the app. It never turns into a payment by itself and there is nothing to cancel. When the 7 days are over, the Pro features lock again until you buy Pro. Nothing you logged is deleted.

How does Tubwise work out a dose?

From your tub’s volume, your latest test and the product you use, with published chemistry:

  • Baking soda, soda ash and calcium chloride by their molar mass.
  • pH down and up with the TroubleFreePool acid model, which takes your alkalinity and borates into account.
  • Chlorine by the available chlorine of each product (dichlor 55.4 %, liquid chlorine by strength), with the CYA or calcium it adds.
  • Ranges from PHTA (via Taylor’s technical FAQ) and the CDC hot tub module.

Then it plays safe: 75 % of each balance step, pH moved at most 0.3 at a time, TA never pushed below 50 ppm by acid, shock never above 10 ppm, and a retest between steps. The free calculators use the same maths and show each formula with its source.

Which test kits does it work with?

Drop kits of the Taylor K-2006, K-2005, K-2106 and K-1515 type (FAS-DPD and DPD), the FAS-DPD syringe, test strips and digital readers or photometers. With strips you tap the value printed on the bottle; there is no camera, so screen colours can’t fool it. The test guide walks through a drop kit.

Litres or gallons? ppm or °dH?

Volume in litres or US gallons (UK gallons if you choose them, shown with litres). Results in ppm. Water reports in °dH or °fH can be typed in and are converted.

Why does it ask for notifications?

Only for the reminders you set: test day, filter care, drain day. They are scheduled on your phone. If you say no, everything else still works.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. Your data exists only on your phone and in any backup files you saved yourself, so that removes it. See the privacy policy.
