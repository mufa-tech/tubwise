Hot tub calculators

Four calculators with the same maths as the Tubwise app. They run in your browser: nothing you type is sent or saved.

  • Chlorine doseDichlor, liquid chlorine or cal-hypo, with the CYA it adds.
  • pH and alkalinitypH down, pH up and baking soda, in safe steps.
  • Drain dayDays between refills, from volume and bather load.
  • Bromine ↔ chlorineConvert a chlorine-scale reading to bromine: × 2.25.
  • Drop-kit test guideRun a full drop-kit test, step by step, before you dose.

Which order to dose in

  1. Total alkalinity first. TA is the buffer that holds pH in place. Fix it with the pH and alkalinity calculator.
  2. Then pH. Wait about 20 minutes with the jets on, test pH again, then adjust it in steps of 0.3 or less.
  3. Then sanitizer. Chlorine works best once pH is in range. Use the chlorine calculator, or the bromine converter if you read bromine on a chlorine scale.

Never add acid and chlorine together or mix them in one bucket. Add each one to the water separately, with the jets running and the cover open.

In the app

The app does the same sums with your own test kit: it asks for each step, gives the doses in order and counts down to drain day. No account, no subscription, works offline.
