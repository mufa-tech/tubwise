Hot tub water test and dosing

Clear water, step by step.

Tubwise walks you through your test kit drop by drop, tells you what to add for your tub and in which order, and counts down to drain day. It works for hot tubs, inflatables, swim spas and cold plunges.

What it does

Your kit, step by step

Pick your kit and Tubwise asks for each step: fill to 25 mL, add the powder, tap once per drop. It does the multiplying. Strips work too: tap the number printed on the bottle, no camera.

Doses in the right order

Alkalinity, then pH, then sanitizer. Grams or ounces for your tub, split into safe steps, with the side effects shown: dichlor adds CYA, acid lowers TA.

Cloudy, foamy, smelly?

Pick the problem. Tubwise checks each likely cause against your last test, marks it confirmed, possible or ruled out, and lists the fix.

Drain day, worked out

A countdown from your real fill date and how often the tub is used. A high TDS reading brings it forward.

Trends you can read

Charts with the target band shaded and every dose marked, so you see when pH creeps up between tests.

Your tub, your sanitizer

Chlorine, bromine, minerals, salt or biguanide. Tubwise never suggests a chemical that clashes with your system.
